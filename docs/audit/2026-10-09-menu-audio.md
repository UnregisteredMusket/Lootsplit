## Behavior and preservation

The existing submenu scene function selects music/ambience: town music with fire for tavern/forge, town music with water by day/crickets by night for market/village, gentle strings/flutes with fire for library/throne room, and mysterious music with dungeon wind/drips for mage study/dungeon. Music follows menu identity even when scenery is hidden or a custom palette is selected. Home, reader, public and account pages have no scene loops. The visual wrapper and main remain mounted; settings changes do not erase drafts.

Separate default-off music/ambient toggles and volumes share the existing device preference record, with safe normalization for older records. Existing SFX controls/previews stay intact. Both settings surfaces expose the same additional controls. Audio starts only after interaction, never during initialization/history playback. Visibility and page lifecycle listeners stop playback; source/version guards reject downloads that complete after cancellation. Volume and same-track menu changes do not restart loops. Playback has two sources and at most three cached decoded files, with no polling/animation timer. Failures do not enter campaign services. Explicit Start / resume can retry blocked or failed playback.

Seven full-length locally bundled MP3s total 3,841,668 bytes. North Fantasy Music's Soft Strings and Flutes / Dark and Mysterious are from the free Fantasy Music and Drum Loops Pack, CC BY 4.0. Town Theme RPG (cynicmusic), Fireplace Sound loop (PagDev), water_flowing from 30 CC0 SFX loops (rubberduck), Crickets Ambient Noise (Ted Kerr/Wolfgang_) and Loopable Dungeon Ambience (JaggedStone) are CC0. Sources verified October9 directly on the creator submission pages. The Resources page, bundled credits, complete license texts and source/converted SHA-256 records preserve attribution and disclosed conversion/edge fades. No paid service or remote playback.
