# Commodity market board

The October 10 request replaces the text-heavy market presentation with a compact commodity board inspired by the user's quote-board and historical trade-list references. This is a shared-client presentation change; it adds no migration, scheduled job, content import, economic rule, private-history permission or distributed Android binary.

Market now has a Global market overview. Trade Exchanges puts searchable quotes and category icons ahead of DM configuration. Each row has explicit physical units, current ask/bid quotes, stock/wanted bars, a recorded price-change indicator and (when available to the DM) a small historical chart. The selected exchange/good shows supply/demand indices, capacity, description, notes, recorded price history and the unchanged authoritative trade form. Desktop uses two columns; phone rows use two compact rows and the selected detail follows the list. Theme tokens preserve light, dark and custom appearances. Existing credited fantasy icons are reused; no new art or third-party content was introduced.

The history reader is presentation-only. It opts into DM history explicitly, validates frozen baselines once per recorded settlement, matches exchange/commodity/unit identities and uses approved applied prices rather than proposals or later DM edits. There is no fabricated price series, volume, order book, volatility, cash balance or trend. A missing prior-season comparison stays unavailable. Lines do not cross unrecorded epochs. Recent 12 is labelled and All recorded exposes the full series; the existing complete seasonal-history and receipt pages remain unchanged.

The board retains visited forms while selecting or filtering other goods, so drafts survive. Current quote fingerprints still reset stale trade forms through the existing key. An exchange directory preserves notes/treasury metadata even for zero-offer exchanges. Every DM editor, offer lock, pending manual review, approved downtime path, property-store destination, reversal review and retry remains reachable. Player availability, private treasury/history, owned account/lot choices and authoritative stock/storage/funds checks are unchanged. Quote links use AppLink and select their target after asynchronous cold hydration without replaying the title during internal navigation.
