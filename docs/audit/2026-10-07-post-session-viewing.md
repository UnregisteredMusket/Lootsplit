## Behavior and preservation

An explicitly opted-in ended room stores `closed: true, viewOnly: true`. Existing legacy/full-closed rooms remain inaccessible to players. Ending archives the current session using the existing archive/report implementation, locks character editing, and keeps existing seats, assignments, sheets, funds, inventory and permitted reports. Current-session activity is preserved in its archived report rather than left in the active-session ledger. Repeating an ambiguously successful viewing-close does not duplicate the report.

The DM disconnects and returns to My account; the room persists without the DM's browser. Existing signed-in players can return via **View campaign** on their account card or the startup chooser, including another device. Guests retain only their existing in-memory data and tab-scoped reconnect ticket; closing/losing that tab is not a durable account membership. New player joins/invitations are not accepted while play has ended. This is not a public room or an offline player copy.

Authorized reads use the same seat-based projection. Other characters' private sheets and DM-only information remain filtered. Campaign edits, purchases, repayments, chat submissions, imports/approvals, new rolls and turn/mode changes are rejected at the server, not just hidden in the UI. Read-only flags disable corresponding client actions, sheet edits/rolls and portrait changes. A remote closure discovered during refresh prevents optimistic mutation. Genuine unsynced local commands and retry identities remain exportable, without being replayed into the ended view or discarded. The previous account-resume acknowledgement/recovery implementation remains intact.

Players may leave their own viewing seat. The DM can use **Close player viewing** from My account to revoke viewing without deleting the campaign or rearchiving it; a revision check prevents a stale close from affecting a reopened session. **Reopen as DM** starts a fresh invitation generation using the existing reopening policy: former player sessions must join the new session; existing revoked tokens do not silently regain access.
