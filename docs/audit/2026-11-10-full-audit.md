# Full audit — November 10, 2026

Baseline: `main` at `2386e62` (#476). Live on both hosts (`lootsplitter.com` and the workers.dev host) is the same commit, release run 42868543155. Every finding cites that commit. Read-only audit: no application code, workflow or data was changed.

## Summary

**79 findings: 6 P0, 9 P1, 34 P2, 30 P3.** 22 need an owner decision. No finding was rejected outright; one (G4) had a wrong number and was corrected.

The five that matter most:

1. **Players can read the names and prices of things the DM has hidden (B1, B2, B3 — P0, reproduced twice).** Activity lines for the DM's ordinary shop, stock, restock, schedule and location edits go to every player, with the shop, item, price and place names in them, even when the shop is a hidden black-market vendor in another city. A coin transfer to a hidden NPC names that NPC in party chat. A barter refusal names any hidden or absent NPC, and imported NPC IDs are sequential, so one visible NPC gives away the rest.
2. **A banned or kicked person can walk back in (B4 — P0, reproduced twice).** Signed out, with the same invitation link, they join as any unclaimed character and can read and post again. The ban is checked only for signed-in accounts and the link is never rotated.
3. **Faction discounts can mint money (A1 — P0, reproduced twice).** A sale is priced at the inverse of the faction buying discount. With the shipped default tiers, one Idolized faction on a "princely" shop (75% buy rate), or two Idolized factions on any default shop, lets a player buy and sell back the same item at a profit every cycle: +69 to +71 cp per 500 cp item. Only finite stock or a merchant purse stops it.
4. **A price that changes under a player is charged without warning (C2 — P1, reproduced twice).** `buy` and `sell` carry no expected price. In Live mode a player was charged 3,000 cp for a rope shown at 100 cp, and 40,000 cp for a different item after a shop-list swap reused the stock line.
5. **Anyone who knows an email address can keep that account from signing in (H3 — P1, reproduced twice).** The sign-in throttle is keyed on email alone. One wrong password every 15 minutes blocked 288 of 288 correct attempts. This contradicts the recorded decision "slow down, don't lock".

Also P0: a shop left on Edit silently puts sold stock and old prices back when the DM tabs through a row (D3, reproduced in a shared room). Also worth knowing now: two client bugs lock the DM out or wipe what they typed (D1, D2), and the main build's changelog stamping is again close to its 240-second limit (G5), which stops every deploy when it trips.
