# Online campaign limit — October 24, 2026 (owner decision)

- **Limit.** Each account may host at most `HOSTED_CAMPAIGN_LIMIT` (default 2) live online campaigns; the site owner (`site_roles.role = 'owner'`) is exempt. "Hosted" is the room's server-set `ownerId`; "live" is a `campaign_rooms` row that is not closed (active, or kept online for viewing after play ends). Closing or deleting a room frees a slot. Joining as a player or guest, account backups, deletion backups and device campaigns (browser and Android) never count; offline play is unlimited.
- **Enforcement (server-side, inside each write).** `cloudflare/hosted-campaigns.mjs` supplies a SQL guard placed in the hosting write itself, so concurrent requests cannot both take the last slot: opening a room (Create room, including moving a device campaign online: `openRoom` → `createRoom`), Reopen as DM (`resumeCampaignMembership`), Restore shared records (`restoreDeletedCampaign`; the backup is never touched) and linking a guest-era DM seat (`linkAccountSeat`). A refusal is a 409 with "You can host up to 2 online campaigns. End or delete one, or keep playing this one offline on your device." Older apps (1.12.0 and earlier) show that server message in their existing error toast. The count reads the partial index `campaign_rooms_live_owner` (migration 0022).
- **Existing accounts over the limit** keep every room playable and visible; only new hosting is refused.
- **UI.** Start a room and My account show "N of 2 online campaigns" (from `library.hosting`); at the limit Create room, Reopen as DM and Restore shared records are disabled with the offline explanation. Refusals are logged to the `campaign-limit` owner monitor source (one-way account tag, no identity).
# Fairness ledger — October 24, 2026 (prompt 2, phase 3)

- **Party → Fairness** (a fourth Party tab for the DM, third for players; owner approved the mockups 2026-10-24). Read-only: how much loot value each party character has received, campaign to date or per session (period selector, `/party?section=fairness&period=<session>`), coins plus items at their recorded value, and an exact gap against the average (rounded half away from zero for display). Tap a character for the breakdown (`&character=<purseId>`): counted receipts newest session first with Activity/session-report links, reversals crossed out beside their reversal, subtotals, and (DM and the character's own player only) the lines on that ledger that do not count. The pool summary card shows who is furthest behind; Split coins hints that an even split never closes a gap.
- **What counts (owner decision).** An allowlist in `src/lib/quire/loot-fairness.ts`: coin split shares and leftovers from a party fund or an encounter, encounter awards (one purse, split, or items straight to a character) and treasure-pool resolutions (`loot.role: "award"`, every choice including DM's choice); reversals subtract what they cancel. Not counted: purchases, sales, trades, Give, wages, loans, payments, downtime, DM adjustments, Add loot, pool additions and a character splitting their own coins. Older encounter items (0 cp lines with no recorded value) count as 0 cp, flagged and listed; never guessed.
- **Who is compared.** Campaign to date: the current party; one session: that session's party (from its archived snapshot). Late joiners show "Joined in Session N". Characters away from the party and former characters are DM-only rows, not compared. NPCs, party funds, non-party accounts and exchange treasuries never appear.
- **Visibility (owner decision, the P1 balance rule).** The DM sees everything; party members see every party character's totals and only the counted receipts of others; a player away from the party sees only their own. Each archived session in a player's projection (`projectRecord`) carries an optional `fairness` extension: that session's still-visible party characters and their counted lines (and reversals) for a seat in the party now, and the split sources of visible lines for everyone. Nothing else is added; the DM's reports carry no extension.
- **Derived, not stored.** No new money state, table or migration. Campaign to date reads the archived session snapshots (migration 0019 storage, already hydrated on every room read); each snapshot is digested once per page load or server isolate (in-memory LRU keyed by the snapshot), the live ledger on each change. Tested with 50 archived sessions.
- **Favour whoever is behind** (Review Inbox → Treasure pool claims; off by default, per resolution; basis campaign to date or this session). Optional `favour: { basis }` on `loot-resolve`; the server computes the totals from the room (never from the client) with the same module, offline through `executeLocalCommand`. Tiers never change (Need beats Greed). Random: only a tie at the cut goes to the lower total; equal totals re-roll as before. Round-robin: each unit to the claimant with the lowest running total (unit values added as they are handed out, across lots), ties keep name order; the rotation pointer still advances. One at a time: not accepted with favour. The preview fingerprint includes the claimants' totals, so a split landing before confirm refuses ("Claims changed. Nothing moved."). The receipt is a separate optional `lootFavour` event field (basis, totals used, who won by favour), filtered by `projectLoot`; older clients ignore it and their journal writes keep it.
- **Award tag.** New encounter-award ledger lines (one-purse coins and items sent straight to a purse) carry an optional `award: { receiptId, role: "coins" | "item", quantity?, unitCopper? }` written by one helper (`src/lib/quire/award-tag.ts`) on both award paths; see [ENCOUNTERS.md](ENCOUNTERS.md). Old lines stay readable.
- **Protocol.** No new field inside a strict tabletop or map schema: ledger lines and journal events are non-strict for older clients, so the tabletop protocol is unchanged.

# Party treasure pool and item claims — October 23, 2026 (prompt 2, phase 2)

- **The pool (owner decision 2026-10-23).** The pool is a party fund's holdings with an optional `pool` marker (`Holding.pool: { at, source: "encounter" | "add-loot" | "dm", encounterId?, receiptId? }`), not a new purse kind, so Funds & inventory, combined wealth, valuations, exports, backups and session reports keep showing pooled items. With no party fund the pool is unavailable (no fund is created automatically). Party → **Treasure pool** (a new tab for the DM and players) lists the lots; Add loot offers **Put in the treasure pool** when the owner is a party fund; encounter loot review offers **Treasure pool · <fund>** per item and **Send all items to the treasure pool**.
- **Guard.** The server refuses Give, sell, NPC barter and exchange offers, blacksmith materials, property storage and equip of a pooled item for everyone, including "Allow party fund" players (`assertNotPooled`/`assertCarried` in `custody.ts`, `validateLootPool`), until the DM resolves it or chooses **Take out of pool** (`loot-pool-set`). A DM patch cannot move a pooled lot out of its fund; an older client that drops the marker keeps it pooled. The DM may still delete a pooled item (its open claims go with it).
- **Claims.** `loot-claim` (Need / Greed / Pass) never moves inventory, coins or ledger lines. Only visible party player characters claim (no NPCs, nobody away from the party); players claim for their own characters, the DM for any party character at a shared table ("marked by the DM"). Claims are visible to the whole party (owner decision), may be sent outside the player's turn in turn-based rooms (sent directly, never staged), and each change is a journal event (`lootClaim`). Open claims live in `journal.lootClaims` (journal patches cannot rewrite them); a player-only device link is read-only.
- **Resolution.** `loot-resolve` (DM only) in Review Inbox → **Treasure pool claims**: one at a time (DM's choice, any party character including someone who passed or a companion NPC), random among Need then Greed (d100 by the room server, or by the DM's device offline, like NPC barter; Pen & Paper totals only under the campaign's manual-roll policy; ties re-roll among the tied characters only), or round-robin with its own `journal.lootClaims.rotation` pointer (pool order by name). A stack with more items than claimants goes round again. Unmarked counts as Pass with a warning; there is no lock step — the preview's fingerprint (`expect`) makes a stale preview fail ("Claims changed. Nothing moved."). One planner (`planLootResolution` in `src/lib/quire/loot-claims.ts`) serves the command, the preview and the tests.
- **Records and exactly once.** One atomic command moves every selected unit (new holding IDs `${commandId}-${lot}-${award}`), writes one receipt (journal event `lootResolution` with claims, unmarked, rolls, awards, kept units and the pointer), a 0 cp `transfer` ledger line per award with `loot: { id, holdingId, role: "award", choice, quantity, unitCopper }` (recorded value for the Phase 3 fairness ledger) and a chat note. Shared rooms dedupe by command ID; offline runs the same engine through `executeLocalCommand` (receipt dedupe in the IndexedDB transaction). Encounter awards to the pool add `role: "added"` lines and a `lootPool` event per item on both paths, inside the existing exactly-once award.
- **Visibility.** DM: everything. Party members: pooled lots, every party claim and claim event, and resolution lines to party players (an NPC award line, its name and the NPC's chat note stay DM-only; the receipt shows as "Treasure pool resolved"). A player away from the party: only their own claims and award lines. Coin balances keep the existing party-only rule. Activity shows each resolution as one row and groups claim changes; sessions list their resolutions; the pool's History tab shows per-item timelines (history moves to session reports at session end and is never truncated). DM Desk "Pending reviews" counts lots ready to resolve.

# Even coin split — October 23, 2026 (prompt 2, phase 1)

- **Split a fund.** Party → Funds & inventory has **Split** on every party fund row and **Split coins** beside Give. The new `coin-split` command (`src/lib/quire/commands.ts`, planner in `coin-split.ts`) divides an amount from one purse evenly among ticked party characters in one atomic action: one receipt (a journal event with a `split` payload), one split ID and one ledger line per moved amount (`split: {id, role}` on each line). Whole copper only: `share = floor(amount / n)`, and shares plus the leftover always equal the amount. The source pays with exact change (`spendCoins`); shares are credited in gold, silver and copper on top of each recipient's own coins, keeping their platinum and electrum. Give is unchanged.
- **Leftover copper (owner decisions 2026-10-22/23).** Goes to the party fund by default (new and existing campaigns): a split from any party fund keeps it in that fund; with no party fund a fund split keeps it in the source and an encounter split gives it to the DM's chosen character (first ticked until chosen). Stays in source, one chosen recipient and rotate (a per-campaign pointer in `journal.coinSplit`) remain options; the DM can save the rule as the campaign default.
- **Who may split.** Whoever may Give from the source: the DM, the controller, or a player allowed to use party funds. Recipients are visible party characters; NPC recipients are DM-only. The server re-plans every split and refuses a stale preview (`expect`). Command IDs make retries apply once, shared and offline (offline runs the same engine through `executeLocalCommand`). Not offered on a player-only device link.
- **Visibility.** Party members see every party member's share and the fund debit (the party-balance rule) and can name the party funds; non-party players see only their own lines. A split that pays an NPC has DM-only receipt text and chat note, and NPC lines are never projected to players (the fund's debit shows a share went elsewhere, which the owner accepted).
- **Encounter awards.** The loot review's **Coins go to** offers One purse (unchanged, original coin types) or **Split evenly among** with the same rules and preview. Awards store optional `coinSplit` (draft) and `coinShares`/`coinRemainder`/`coinSplitId` (receipt); both the shared Worker (`cloudflare/encounters.mjs`) and device (`src/lib/encounters/local.ts`) paths use one planner and keep exactly-once. Awards with a single `coinPurseId` read and display exactly as before.
- **Activity and journal.** Activity shows each split as one expandable row (fund or encounter), session records list the session's splits, and fund splits post one party chat note.

- **Client error feedback (P5, #180).** See the entry under Cross-device campaign selection.
- **Room polling cost (P6, #173).** Unchanged polls keep the same snapshot and notify no subscribers; the recovery readout is cached; a pending-action preview conflict is shown once and backs off instead of reloading every 2 s.
