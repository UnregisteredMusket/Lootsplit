Lootsplit uses eight selected day/night scene pairs, resized from 1920x1080 JPEGs
to 768x432 and 1440x810 WebP, quality 77. Artwork is bundled locally for web
and Android; no third-party image request is required. See sources.json for original
filenames, archive hashes, adaptations and per-file integrity hashes.

The original creator discloses AI generation with Krea 2, then Real-ESRGAN
upscaling. Their unmodified pack documentation is SOURCE-README.md.
